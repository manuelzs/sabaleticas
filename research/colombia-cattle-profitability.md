# Colombian cattle profitability — overview & synthesis

**Operation:** ~200 ha *ceba de hembras* in La Pintada, suroeste de Antioquia (tierra
caliente, ~550–600 m, Cauca valley). ~266 head, 96% female, plus calves born on-farm
from pregnant purchases. **Losing money monthly.**

This is the entry point to the research library. The **detail lives in the topic
documents** ([`topics/`](topics/)); every claim cites the **source register**
([`sources.md`](sources.md)) by `[S#]`. First synthesized 2026-05-21.

## The 5 highest-leverage takeaways for Sabaleticas

1. **The price spread is the whole game, and it works against us twice.** $/kg *falls*
   as cattle get heavier (levante ~$10,013 > gordo ~$9,021/kg, mid-2025) **and** females
   sell at a ~10–15% discount to males. A *ceba de hembras* fights both headwinds — margin
   must come from the **buy-spread + cheap kilos of gain**, never the sale price.
   → [`topics/economics-and-benchmarks.md`](topics/economics-and-benchmarks.md),
   [`topics/ceba-de-hembras.md`](topics/ceba-de-hembras.md)
2. **We're almost certainly under-stocked AND under-gaining.** The national-*average*
   (money-losing) farm runs ~0.87 animal/ha, ~0.35 kg/day, slaughter at ~40 months.
   Profitable tropical operations hit 1–2/ha, 0.5–0.85 kg/day, finish in 12–14 months.
   1.33 head/ha with no weighing is the losing baseline.
   → [`topics/economics-and-benchmarks.md`](topics/economics-and-benchmarks.md)
3. **Pasture is the #1 lever and mostly free.** Rotational grazing on improved Brachiaria
   ≈ 3× kg/ha vs. continuous; silvopastoral (Leucaena) pushes GDP to 600–900 g/day.
   → [`topics/pasture-forage-grazing.md`](topics/pasture-forage-grazing.md)
4. **It's a velocity business — monthly return tracks turnover, not herd size.** Long
   cycles double fixed cost per kg sold. Don't cut the cheap inputs (health <1%, nutrition
   ~4%) that unlock gain.
   → [`topics/economics-and-benchmarks.md`](topics/economics-and-benchmarks.md),
   [`topics/failure-modes-and-fixes.md`](topics/failure-modes-and-fixes.md)
5. **Sell weighed, at auction.** Farm-gate to a comisionista leaves money on the table;
   Feria de Medellín leads national gordo prices and is nearby.
   → [`topics/selling-and-channels.md`](topics/selling-and-channels.md)

## Glossary (Colombian terms)

*ceba* = fattening/finishing · *levante* = growing phase (weaning→~330 kg) · *cría* =
cow-calf breeding · *ciclo completo* = all three · *GDP / ganancia diaria de peso* = avg
daily gain (kg/day) · *potrero* = paddock · *aforo* = forage-mass/carrying-capacity
estimate · *carga animal* = stocking rate · *UGG/UA* = ~450–500 kg animal-equivalent ·
*en pie* = liveweight · *flaco/a* = thin (feeder) · *gordo/a* = finished/fat · *novillo/a*
= steer/heifer · *vaca de descarte* = cull cow · *invierno/verano* = rainy/dry season
(rain, not temperature) · *rendimiento en canal* = carcass dressing % · *subasta/feria* =
auction · *comisionista* = broker · *frigorífico* = slaughter plant.

## Standing caveats

- Hard cost/financial structure is AGROSAVIA **Orinoquía** data `[S1]` — no Antioquia ceba
  study found (Antioquia's 2017 cost/kg was actually *lower*, so possibly more favorable).
  Re-derive % from Sabaleticas' own books.
- Price points are 2024–2025 snapshots `[S4][S5]` and drift; the *relationships* (levante >
  gordo, male > female, ~3× kg/ha from rotation) are the durable part.
- No Colombian ceba **mortality** benchmark sourced — flagged, not invented.
- Some silvopastoral figures are Brazilian proxies, labeled in the topic doc.

## The price-spread trap (the core of the business)

Ceba profit = `(kg sold × sale $/kg) − (kg bought × purchase $/kg) − cost of kilos added − overhead`.

The naïve model ("buy at X $/kg, add weight, sell at X $/kg, pocket the gain") is
**wrong in Colombia** because **$/kg falls as the animal gets heavier**:

- Macho de **levante** (lighter): ~$10,013/kg · Macho **gordo** (finished): ~$9,021/kg (mid-2025) `[S4]`
- So a steer can be worth **~$1,000/kg LESS** as gordo than as levante. Every added kilo sells into a *falling* price.

Your margin is therefore the **buy/sell spread on the original frame** + **value of added kilos at the lower finished price − what those kilos cost to add**. If pasture is poor and the animal sits 18–24 months at 0.35 kg/day, the cost of those kilos eats the spread. `[S1][S4]`

## Cost structure (AGROSAVIA Orinoquía case, 2018 — proxy) `[S1]`

| Cost item | % of total |
|---|---|
| Animal purchase | 41.2% |
| Land / pasture rent | 25.3% |
| Temporary labor | 18.0% |
| Pasture maintenance | 6.3% |
| Nutrition (salt, silage, supplement) | 4.2% |
| Sanidad (health/vet) | **0.8%** |
| Transport / commercialization / admin / equip. | ~4.2% |

**Animal purchase + land rent ≈ 66% of all cost.** Levers: (a) buy well, (b) own/cheapen
land or extract far more kg per hectare (carga + GDP), (c) keep labor productive.
**Health is <1% — cutting sanidad/nutrition to "save" is false economy**; those cheap
line items unlock the GDP the whole margin depends on. `[S1]`

## Turnover is decisive (velocity > size)

Same land/labor/finance spread over fewer sold animals = fixed cost per kg sold roughly
doubles. The profitable AGROSAVIA cases: the **shortest, highest-GDP cycle (0.85 kg/day,
~9 months) had the best MONTHLY return (~4.4%)** even though a long cycle had a higher
headline total %. For a money-losing operation, **monthly return — i.e. velocity — is
what matters.** `[S1]`

| AGROSAVIA case | GDP (kg/d) | Duration | Rentabilidad mensual |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caso 1 | 0.75–0.85 | 9–11 mo | **4.4%** |
| Caso 2 | 0.43 | 13 mo | 3.9% |
| Caso 3 | 0.47 | 17 mo | 3.5% |

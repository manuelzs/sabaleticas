# Why Colombian operations lose money — failure modes & fixes

> Living doc. Sources cited as `[S#]` → see [`../sources.md`](../sources.md).
> Consistent across AGROSAVIA, Fedegán, CONtexto.

## Recurring failure modes

1. **Continuous free grazing, low carga** (~0.87/ha vs. ~2/ha achievable) → overgrazing, degraded Brachiaria, low GDP. *The #1 structural loss.* `[S6][S1]`
2. **Low turnover** — 20–40 month cycles spread fixed cost over too few sales/year; monthly return collapses. `[S1]`
3. **No weighing / no records ("a memoria")** — most farms use the báscula only at sale, not as a management tool. *"Si no hay información, no hay nada."* You can't manage GDP or cost/kg you don't measure. `[S7]`
4. **Adverse price spread mismanaged** — buying heavy, or selling into the gordo/female $/kg discount without working the buy-spread. `[S4][S10]`
5. **Subscale fixed costs** — land rent (~25%) and labor (~18%) don't shrink with a half-empty pasture; under-stocking is doubly punishing. `[S1]`
6. **Informal farm-gate selling to comisionistas** — no price discovery, no scale, value captured by intermediaries. `[S6]`
7. **False economy on cheap inputs** — cutting sanidad (<1%) and nutrition (~4%) starves the GDP the whole margin depends on; no dry-season ration plan → weight loss in verano. `[S1][S15]`

## Highest-leverage fixes (in order of ROI) `[S6][S7][S16][S1]`

1. **Subdivide potreros and rotate** (occupation 2–4 d / rest 28–33 d). Low cash cost, immediate GDP + carga + kg/ha gain. Set carga via *aforo* `[S16]`.
2. **Buy a báscula; keep per-animal records** (entry weight, periodic weights ~every 56 days, cost per animal). Manage to GDP and cost/kg gained.
3. **Renovate/fertilize degraded pasture; plant improved Brachiaria/Cynodon**, then move toward **SSPi with Leucaena** (Fedegán GCS) for shade + 600–900 g/day + 3–5/ha.
4. **Compress the cycle to ≤12–14 months** with finishing supplement + dry-season silage; raise turnover.
5. **Push carga toward farm capacity** (fix pasture first) — likely well above 266 head once pasture is improved.
6. **Improve selling channel & price discovery** → see [`selling-and-channels.md`](selling-and-channels.md).
7. **Run real costos de producción** (AGROSAVIA's manual is a free template `[S1]`): track variable vs fixed, compute c/b, VPN, TIR. Target **TIR > 12%, c/b > 1**.

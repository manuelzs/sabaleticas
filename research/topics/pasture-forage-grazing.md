# Pasture, forage & grazing — the #1 lever

> Living doc. Sources cited as `[S#]` → see [`../sources.md`](../sources.md).
> This is the highest-ROI area for Sabaleticas (hypothesis H2 in the diagnosis).

## Grasses for tierra caliente Antioquia (La Pintada climate)

- **Brachiaria/Urochloa** — *brizantha* (Toledo/Marandú), *decumbens*, hybrids **Mulato II**, **Cayman**. The workhorse improved tropical grass: ~8% crude protein, ~65% digestibility; *brizantha* can yield ~5,000 kg DM/ha per cycle with ~36-day rest. `[S1][S12]`
- **Cynodon (estrella, *C. nlemfuensis*)** — high gain: ~680 g/day vs. Mulato II ~500 g/day in one tropical grazing study. `[S13]`
- **Megathyrsus/Panicum maximum (guinea, Tanzania, Mombasa)** — high-yield, good in fertile hot lowland; used in silvopastoral with Leucaena.

## Rotational grazing (the highest-ROI change, low cash cost)

- **Continuous grazing** (one big undivided potrero) → overgrazing, selective grazing, wasted "islas", degraded pasture, <1 animal/ha, low GDP. **This is the default money-losing pattern.** `[S6]`
- **Rotational**: subdivide into potreros, **~2–4 days occupation / ~28–33 days rest** for Brachiaria. Raises forage quantity/quality, lets carga rise to **1/ha (verano)–2/ha (invierno)**, lifts GDP to ~15–20 kg/animal/month. `[S12][S16]`
- **Productivity jump:** intensified rotational Brachiaria reached **~960–1,000 kg carne/ha/yr vs. ~350** traditional — roughly **3×**. `[S12]`

## Aforo / forage budget (presupuesto forrajero)

Measure kg DM/ha available, divide by intake per animal → set how many animals a
potrero holds and for how long. The discipline most farms skip ("introduce animals
without control into enormous areas"). For Sabaleticas: **set carga from data, don't
eyeball it.** `[S16]`

## Silvopastoral systems (SSP/SSPi) — medium-term play `[S11][S9]`

Trees/shrubs (esp. **Leucaena leucocephala**, 10,000+ plants/ha) + improved grass:
- GDP **~400 → 600–900 g/day**; carga **~3–5 animales/ha**; forage +27–63%; protein intake +148%. `[S9]`
- Finishing ~26 months; **shade is critical in tierra caliente** (heat stress cuts intake/GDP); drought resilience, soil, carbon credits.
- **Fedegán "Ganadería Colombiana Sostenible" (GCS)** (with CIPAV/TNC) is the institutional vehicle — may offer technical/financial support. `[S11]`
- Caveat: higher establishment cost, 1–2 yr setup. Not the immediate fix.

## Dry-season (verano) management

Carga is seasonal (~2/ha invierno, ~1/ha verano). Profitable farms bridge verano with
**ensilaje (silage)** and finishing supplement (0.5–2 kg concentrate/animal/day) to
**hold carga and GDP through the dry season** instead of losing the year's gains.
La Pintada's Cauca-valley verano is where unmanaged herds lose weight. `[S1][S15]`

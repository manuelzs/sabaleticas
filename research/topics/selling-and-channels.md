# Selling channels & price discovery

> Living doc. Sources cited as `[S#]` → see [`../sources.md`](../sources.md).

## Channels, worst → best for price capture

- **Comisionista / intermediario at farm gate** — most common, least price discovery; "ganaderos venden a intermediarios y muy pocos llegan a los mercados finales." Convenient, but you take the broker's price, often **without independent weighing**. `[S6]`
- **Subasta / feria ganadera (auction)** — transparent competitive price discovery; **weighed**; published averages let you benchmark. **Typically beats farm-gate.** `[S4]`
- **Frigorífico / venta en canal (direct to plant)** — captures more of the chain (also sell vísceras/subproductos), but needs volume, logistics, and knowing your animal's chain value. Higher complexity.

## Price discovery matters — the spread is wide

Same week, same category, prices varied **~$1,000/kg** across auctions/regions
(mid-2025 macho gordo: $9,600 Caucasia → $10,308 Medellín). Knowing the published
Fedegán/CONtexto series and choosing where/when to sell is **free margin**. `[S2][S4]`

**Feria de Medellín consistently leads national gordo prices** — a strong, nearby
market for an Antioquia operation. `[S4]`

## Timing

Prices are cyclical/seasonal (retention/extraction cycles; dry-season supply swings).
2024–2025 was a strong bull market (macho gordo ~$4,400 in 2019 → ~$8,000–9,000+ in
2024–2025). Sell **finished** animals into strength; **avoid forced dry-season sales of
underweight cattle**. `[S4][S2]`

> **Rule for a money-losing farm:** don't sell on the broker's schedule. Sell
> **weighed**, into the best-discovered price, ideally at a strong auction (Medellín).

# Financials

The money side, in COP. For a **ceba** operation the central question is the
**margin on the kilos gained** — so we build outward from there: per-lote margin
first (see [`../herd/lotes/`](../herd/lotes/)), then the cost structure that feeds it,
then the whole-operation P&L and break-even.

## To build here

- `pnl.md` — revenue and expenses, by period
- `costs.md` — cost structure broken into categories (feed/supplements, animal
  health, labor, pasture/land, machinery, admin, finance)
- `break-even.md` — cost per head, cost per kg produced, break-even sale price
- `balance-sheet.md` — assets (land, herd, equipment) and liabilities
- `revenue.md` — sales records, prices realized, by channel

## Principles

- All figures dated and sourced (which document/period they came from).
- Separate **cash** reality from **accrual/economic** reality (e.g. herd value
  changes, depreciation) — both matter, they answer different questions.
- The herd is both inventory and capital; track its value movement explicitly.

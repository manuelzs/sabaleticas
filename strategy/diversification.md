# Diversification — alternative uses of the land & capital

> **Status: parked for future discussion** (raised by owner, 2026-05-25). No numbers yet —
> this file just holds the idea so it isn't lost. Nothing here is researched or decided.

## The question

The core project is fixing the cattle operation (see [`../financials/diagnosis.md`](../financials/diagnosis.md)).
Diversification asks the bigger one **sitting above that:** is *ceba* the best use of this
~200 ha and the capital tied up in it — or would part (or all) of the land earn more in
another use? A losing cattle operation on valuable, well-located land (road frontage on
Ruta 25, river access — see [`../operations/land/location.md`](../operations/land/location.md))
makes the **opportunity cost** of the land a real question, not just the cattle margin.

## Leads raised by the owner (anecdotal — unverified)

- **Citrus.** A neighbor has had **good results** planting citrus. (No figures yet.)
- **Solar panel farm.** Another neighbor has **explored** a solar farm. (No figures yet —
  unknown if it went ahead.)

Both are **neighbors' experiences**, so they share our climate, soils, and access — which
makes them more relevant proxies than generic studies, but they're still hearsay until we get
real numbers.

## Why it's worth considering

- The cattle operation is losing money monthly; if a turnaround can't clear a low bar, the
  land's value may be better realized another way.
- Citrus and solar are **very different risk/return/effort profiles** from cattle and from
  each other (citrus = multi-year orchard establishment, perishable crop, labor; solar =
  capital-heavy, needs grid access + a buyer/PPA, long contracts, low ongoing labor). They
  may also be **partial** — e.g. solar or citrus on part of the land while cattle continues
  on the rest.

## What we'd need to evaluate it (when we pick it up)

Same discipline as the cattle side — compare on a **return-per-hectare and return-on-capital**
basis, against the cattle baseline once that baseline exists:

- **Cattle baseline first.** We can't judge alternatives without knowing what cattle actually
  earns (or loses) per hectare — that comes from the P&L, which is still pending the owner's
  records ([`../data-inventory.md`](../data-inventory.md)). So this naturally **follows** the
  diagnosis, not precedes it.
- **Citrus:** establishment cost/ha, years to first harvest, yield, price, water needs
  (ties to the [water-management topic](../operations/land/location.md)), labor, which
  citrus and rootstock suit ~550–600 m tierra caliente.
- **Solar:** is grid interconnection feasible near Ruta 25? capex, who buys the power (PPA),
  land area vs. revenue, lease-vs-own models, contract length.
- Get the **neighbors' actual numbers** if they'll share — far more useful than national data.

## How this fits priority

**Does not jump the queue.** The immediate work is still the cattle P&L and turnaround
diagnosis. Diversification is the strategic fork we evaluate *with* that baseline in hand —
important, but downstream of getting real cattle numbers.
